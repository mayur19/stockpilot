# Risk register

Likelihood and impact are rated **L**ow / **M**edium / **H**igh. Exposure is the product of the
two (H×H = critical). Owners are roles, since the team is small. The register is reviewed at
the end of every milestone. Status is one of `open`, `mitigating`, `mitigated` (controls
in place and tested) or `closed`.

| ID   | Risk                                                                                                                                                   | L   | I   | Mitigation (in place → planned)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Owner         | Status     |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --- | --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ---------- |
| R-01 | **Overselling**: two concurrent allocations reserve the same units, so an order is promised stock that does not exist.                                 | M   | H   | Candidate rows are locked `FOR UPDATE` in one statement ordered by `(sku_id, id)`. The plan is computed in pure code against the locked snapshot. `CHECK (allocated <= on_hand)` is a backstop. The no-oversell race test (12 orders, 39 units) runs in CI. → Load test under hot-SKU contention (SP-181).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Tech lead     | mitigated  |
| R-02 | **Ledger/projection drift**: `stock_balances` diverges from `inventory_movements` (manual SQL, a partial restore, a bug in one write path).            | M   | H   | The projection is updated in the same transaction as the append. The ledger is append-only (trigger). `GET /inventory/projection/verify` replays and diffs, and drift injection is tested. The runbook has a correction procedure. → Snapshots to keep verify cheap (SP-044), scheduled verification with alerts (SP-180).                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Tech lead     | mitigating |
| R-03 | **UoM errors**: case vs each confusion, rounding, or a ladder change that alters historical meaning (e.g. CS 12 → 24).                                 | M   | H   | All quantities are stored in base units as safe integers. Ladders must be nested. Conversion refuses fractional results. The receipt response returns a breakdown that a human can check. Randomised round-trip tests. → Versioned ladders, so history keeps its ladder (SP-035).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Domain lead   | mitigating |
| R-04 | **Pick path performance**: the 2-opt optimiser is too slow for large waves, or its routes are worse than the serpentine default.                       | M   | M   | The serpentine `pickSequence` is a working fallback today. → Time-boxed optimiser with a fallback to the S-shape/largest-gap heuristic. A benchmark suite of layouts with p95 < 200 ms for 100 stops. A brute-force oracle on small cases (SP-100, ADR-0017).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Tech lead     | open       |
| R-05 | **Cartonization quality**: the 3D heuristic chooses oversized boxes (freight cost) or impossible packings (pack station rework).                       | H   | M   | → Item dimensions required before cartonization (SP-034). Property tests for geometry validity. Fill-ratio benchmark against a baseline. Packers can override with a scan (SP-112, ADR-0018).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Domain lead   | open       |
| R-06 | **Multi-instance throttles**: running two API processes splits the in-memory login counters, multiplying the attacker's guess budget.                  | M   | H   | Single-instance constraint documented in deployment.md. Production requires an explicit `TRUST_PROXY`. Stores are bounded, and evicted active lock-outs overflow into a fixed sketch. → SP-183 and SP-190 (Postgres-backed shared store) before any horizontal scaling.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Security lead | mitigating |
| R-07 | **Spoofed client IP** via `X-Forwarded-For` bypasses the per-IP limits, or a misconfigured proxy makes all users share one IP and lock each other out. | M   | H   | `TRUST_PROXY=true` is rejected. Only hop counts or explicit CIDRs are accepted, and production refuses an unset value. The nginx template overwrites `X-Forwarded-For`. Integration tests cover the trusted and untrusted cases.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Security lead | mitigated  |
| R-08 | **Throttle store exhaustion**: a flood of random emails or IPs fills memory or evicts a victim's lock-out.                                             | M   | M   | Precise caches (authoritative) capped at 100k with sweep-then-evict and a warning log. Only force-evicted active per-email entries are absorbed into a fixed ~1 MiB HMAC-keyed count-min sketch, which is read only for uncached emails and ignored once a row is more than 50% full. Distinct-email caps per IPv4 address, IPv6 /64 and /48. Unit tests: an evicted lock-out survives a flood, spread failures never lock out an untouched email, and a successful login always clears. Saturation is logged as `throttle_sketch_saturated`. | Security lead | mitigated  |
| R-09 | **Expired or short-dated stock shipped** because of time zone mistakes (a UTC "today" vs the warehouse's local date).                                  | L   | H   | Expiry is a calendar date. "Today" is computed in the warehouse's canonical IANA zone (`todayIn`). Expired and short-shelf-life lots are excluded in the pure planner. Boundary tests exist.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Domain lead   | mitigated  |
| R-10 | **Allocation hot spots**: one fast-moving SKU serialises all allocators on its rows, so latency spikes during waves.                                   | M   | M   | Locks cover only the rows with available stock, in short transactions. Deadlocks map to `CONCURRENCY_RETRY` (503). → Wave-level batch allocation (SP-093), load test (SP-181), and if needed, splitting pick-face and reserve rows (SP-053).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Tech lead     | open       |
| R-11 | **Ledger growth** makes replay, verify and movement queries slow as years of movements accumulate.                                                     | H   | M   | Keyset pagination with `(organization_id, warehouse_id, id DESC)` index. → Snapshots (SP-044), monthly partitioning (SP-186), point-in-time queries from snapshots (SP-043).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Tech lead     | open       |
| R-12 | **Lot master data conflicts**: the same lot code arrives with different expiries, or untracked SKUs collapse many receipts into one lot.               | M   | M   | `LOT_EXPIRY_MISMATCH` (409) rejects the conflict. Untracked SKUs get one `AUTO-YYYYMMDD` lot per warehouse-local day, so FIFO still has an age. → ASN-level lot validation (SP-071).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Domain lead   | mitigated  |
| R-13 | **Data loss on restore**: backups exist but restores are untested or inconsistent with the ledger.                                                     | M   | H   | `pg_dump` custom-format procedure, restore into a new database, verify after restore (runbook). → Automated monthly drill (SP-182), optional WAL archiving.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Operations    | mitigating |
| R-14 | **Irreversible migration** breaks production with no down-migration available.                                                                         | L   | H   | Forward-only runner with checksums and one transaction per file. Additive and backwards-compatible migrations only. Backup before every release. Code rollback by symlink (deployment.md).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Tech lead     | mitigated  |
| R-15 | **Scope and estimate risk**: about 1 394 h of pending work, with algorithmic tickets (SP-100, SP-111, SP-080) likely to overrun.                       | H   | M   | Foundation-first delivery. Every ticket estimated. Pending routes return 501 with tickets so the scope is visible. → Re-estimate at each milestone exit; spike algorithmic tickets first in their milestone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Product owner | open       |

Tracked in roadmap.md.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Product owner | open       |
| R-17 | **Physical vs system mismatch** from unrecorded moves (stock moved without scanning), which the ledger cannot detect.                                  | H   | M   | Moves only through the API with an audit trail and actor. → Blind cycle counts with approval (SP-130..132), scan-verified tasks (SP-081, SP-101), labour accountability (SP-188).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Operations    | open       |

Security headers. → ASVS L2 review (SP-183).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Security lead | mitigated  |
